A cosmetics brand based in Italy with retail in France + Germany. Building a luxury beauty brand with limited distribution requires a different approach to growth.

For L’Une, TMB developed a targeted organic and low-boost social strategy, pairing thoughtful brand storytelling with elevated, editorial creative produced by our team.

### Strategic Outcomes
- **Grew Social Community to 2K+ Highly Engaged Followers** despite strictly niche geographical availability.
- **Formulated a High-End Digital Visual Identity** engineered to scale alongside its expanding European department store and boutique presence.
- **Elevated Editorial Asset Suite**: Produced on-location photography and video content reflecting European high-fashion aesthetics.
