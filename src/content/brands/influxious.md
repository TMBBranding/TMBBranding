Founded by licensed therapist and fragrance expert Funmi Monet, Influxious is a modern fragrance house rooted in Nigerian heritage, intentional self-expression, and the belief that fragrance is an extension of identity.

TMB joined Influxious at a pivotal stage of growth, helping shape the brand’s social strategy and translate its distinct point of view into an editorial world built for community, culture, and discovery.

### Highlights & Growth
- **Multi-Channel Founder Storytelling**: Blending olfactory education, emotional wellness, and cultural celebration.
- **Sephora Expansion**: Designed a digital presence that kept Funmi’s voice at the center while successfully scaling onto Sephora shelves.
- **High-Engagement Cultural Community**: Uniting fragrance enthusiasts and cultural tastemakers through viral UGC and private salon events.
