Founded by licensed therapist and fragrance expert Funmi Monet, Influxious is a modern fragrance house rooted in Nigerian heritage, intentional self-expression, and the belief that fragrance is an extension of identity.

TMB joined Influxious at a pivotal stage of growth, shaping the brand’s social strategy and translating its distinct point of view into an editorial world built for community, culture, and discovery.

From founder storytelling and fragrance education to UGC, community events, and the brand’s expansion onto Sephora shelves, we’ve helped build a social presence designed to grow alongside the brand while keeping Funmi’s voice and story at the center. Through a broader growth strategy that paired organic storytelling with strategic content boosting, organic social accounted for up to 40% of total site traffic in a single week.
