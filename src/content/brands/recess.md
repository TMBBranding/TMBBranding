Recess is transforming how parents discover and book kids’ camps, classes, and activities — and we’ve been along for the ride since before day one.

From launching the brand in Austin to supporting its expansion into Dallas and Houston, TMB has helped build Recess into a growing community of families, creators, providers, and partners. We’ve developed and executed the social strategy from the ground up, growing the community to 20K+ followers while leading content creation, influencer and UGC partnerships, market launches, brand collaborations, ambassador and referral programs, and community activations — all designed to turn awareness into discovery and bookings.
