Managing both consumer and professional audiences required a strategy that could balance education, inspiration, and commerce without losing sight of the brand.

Tiffany led social strategy across both channels, rebuilding momentum and strengthening social as a meaningful driver of community and conversion.

- **Grew Brand’s Instagram Communities by 170K+ Followers**.
- **+115% MoM Increase in Instagram-Driven Sales**.
- **+517% MoM Increase in Pinterest-Driven Sales**.
- **Increased Social Reach by 25%, Impressions by 9%, and Engagement by 29%**.

By reestablishing social commerce alongside a stronger content strategy, we helped turn the brand’s digital channels into both an iconic storytelling platform and a measurable sales driver.
