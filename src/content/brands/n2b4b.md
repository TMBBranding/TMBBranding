A case study in turning founder influence into lasting brand momentum, N2B4B launched with more than 17K followers in its first month — entirely organically. TMB partnered with Francelys from pre-launch to build anticipation, shape the brand story, and create a community that extends beyond the founder herself.

Since launch, we’ve supported N2B4B through product and retail launches, founder storytelling, education, UGC, events, and community-driven content — helping the brand reach nearly 200K accounts in a single month and grow beyond 20K Instagram followers.

Today, N2B4B is available at REVOLVE and FWRD, with brand-led content increasingly standing among its strongest performers.
