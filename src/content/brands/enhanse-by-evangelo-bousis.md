Founded by Evangelo Bousis, Enhanse is bringing a fresh perspective to men’s skincare. TMB joined the brand at the beginning of its next chapter, reimagining its social strategy and shaping how its story comes to life online.

Our work includes social strategy, paid boosting, ambassador program strategy, founder positioning, and community management - all designed to build a distinct presence for the brand and connect with the men (and women) it serves.

Since we began working together, Enhanse has earned coverage in _WWD_ and launched at Nordstrom. We’re thrilled to be part of a brand experiencing such exciting early momentum and to help shape what comes next.
